# Auto Adjust boundary-noise handling — 1.12.2

The previous boundary test accepted three occupied cells with spread along just one other axis, and placed the face using the outermost point. A small connected trash patch or a thin streak could therefore expand a box even after ground exclusion.

Auto Adjust now requires a connected patch of at least six occupied 8 cm cells in a 6 cm axial band. Connections are limited to 24 cm. Both directions of the face need a minimum span of min(15 cm, 15% of the corresponding box dimension), and one needs min(25 cm, 25% of the dimension). Duplicated raw returns cannot increase occupied-cell confidence. The median position of the supported patch replaces the single outermost return; existing 3 cm padding remains.

A face without this evidence is retained. If inward movement would pass a group of at least three uncertain returns beyond the patch/padding, the face is also retained: those returns might represent a real thin part. Distant-face shrink rejection, saved distance/percentage limits, slope-aware ground exclusion, upright restriction, fixed heading, ground contact and native one-edit/no-op paths remain active. The adapter reports when weak boundary candidates were ignored. Auto-Fit and Move to mouse geometry are unchanged.

Validation: 113 unit tests and 31 Edge browser tests passed. Added regressions cover three-point side/top trash patches, close-to-body outliers, 100 repeated returns per point, linear streaks, compact patches, disconnected scattered returns, rotated boxes, real six-return supported extensions, uncertain thin geometry, and shrink protection around possibly real protrusions. The browser regression injects duplicated above-ground trash through the native adapter bridge, compares the resulting geometry against the clean cloud, and checks one-save behavior. Existing ground/no-op/occlusion/undo-delegation, camera, keyboard, session and batch tests passed. Extension/demo bundles rebuilt; packaged ZIP contents verified against sources.

The user's screenshot motivated these synthetic regressions. Its exact cloud and before/after geometry were not available, so that scene has not been replayed or certified. A sufficiently large coherent background patch can still resemble an object surface; the tool does not perform semantic recognition. Very sparse legitimate boundaries may remain unchanged and need manual adjustment. Authenticated live CVAT acceptance remains pending.
